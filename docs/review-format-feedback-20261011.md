# Bounded format feedback for technical reviews

Full native trial 24 produced twelve chapters, but failed before delivery.
Its coordinated creative review omitted the original chapter from individual
check citations. Later, its issue reconciliation twice tried to close findings
without citing every affected chapter. Both roles retried the original request
without the validator's errors. The second call could not identify what the
first call had omitted.

Technical correctness reviewers now receive specific deterministic errors on
their existing second format attempt. An error names the chapter, the check and
the missing source. Issue reconciliation may leave an allegation open when the
evidence cannot justify closing it. No extra attempts are granted, and a valid
negative judgment remains final. Blind literary readers and preference voters
receive no new feedback.

The retry receives the complete original assignment and format errors only.
It does not receive the previous review, its prose judgments or a desired score.
Original failed responses and their native receipts remain on disk. Completed
steps bind the failed packet, response, transport outputs, receipt and validation
record as evidence. Group publication reconstructs the exact retry packet and
checks its hash against the native receipt. Altering the failed evidence also
invalidates completed replay.

Regression coverage includes both factual and creative group reviews that omit
specific evidence, an issue check that correctly keeps an allegation open,
unchanged completed replay, and rejection of tampered failed responses. The
existing provider-wait and producer-repair behavior retains its bounded attempt
and evidence rules. See the dated local validation ledger for the full suite
and native test outcomes; component checks do not certify a complete book or
literary quality above 90/100.

The failed full-book run is not resumed on changed source, its attempts are not
refunded, and its score is not replaced. New native component tests and books
use a separately frozen source revision.
