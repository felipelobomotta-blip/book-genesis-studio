## 3. Writing-quality enforcement

### What the pipeline actually does

The phase order is fixed in `skills/book-genesis/references/pipeline/phases.md`: intake, foundation, architecture, drafting, adversarial audit, revision, final score, editorial package. Phase 4 is never skipped. Scores come after the audit. `skills/book-genesis/references/scoring/genesis-score.md` tells the orchestrator to keep the rubric and the targets away from critics.

Gates that are implemented, not merely described:

- Length contract from the genre table in `skills/book-genesis/references/patterns/index.md`, copied into project state at intake. A manuscript under the floor is not called complete unless intake recorded a short form.
- Plan checks for premise, promise, timeline and point of view before drafting, and again at the final audit (changelog and `docs/plan-chapter-verification-20261011.md`).
- Continuity ledger and factual checks. Narrow factual repairs are separate from creative preference (`docs/factual-corrections-20261010.md`).
- Four simulated readers and a ten-dimension rubric. The reader gate keeps a revision only when the recorded preference says so. The rubric gate uses a calibrated floor. Default `quality_target` is 8.5 on a 0–9.2 scale (`skills/book-genesis/scripts/autonomous_book.py`). The gate also fails when any dimension sits more than 1.0 below the target (`genesis-score.md`). Missing evidence stays incomplete. A passing preference vote does not close a continuity defect.
- Beta 8 schedule version 3: after a valid negative group review, independent units still need a fresh current-book check and a fresh four-reader vote before publication (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-independent-revisions.md`).
- Export is separate from literary acceptance. An EPUB can exist while the gates are unmet, and the delivery record has to say so.

### Where the practices come from

`skills/book-genesis/references/patterns/index.md` is the index. It points at published craft sources: Blatt, *Nabokov's Favorite Word Is Mauve* (2017); Brody, *Save the Cat! Writes a Novel* (2018); Career Authors and Reedsy word-count guides; Archer and Jockers, *The Bestseller Code* (2016); Reagan et al., EPJ Data Science (2016); and the other works named in that index and in `structure-and-emotion.md`. The index states that the thresholds measure craft risk and do not predict sales. It also withdraws two unsourced sales claims that used to travel with the library.

Those files are summaries and project checks. They are not copies of the cited books.

### Dataset and provenance

There is no top-book text dataset in this repository, and no license to one.

- `skills/book-genesis/references/research/english-fiction-metadata-20261011.json` holds seven manually checked US English publisher-metadata records, dated 2026-10-11. The file's own scope says it is not a bestseller ranking, a whole-book reading, or a measured prose corpus. `docs/research-snapshot-20261011.md` says the same. Page counts are edition facts, not word counts.
- Timezone data under `skills/book-genesis/scripts/data/` is IANA tzdata, with its license files. It is not book text.
- No training run on a bestseller corpus is recorded. Saying the plugin was "trained on top books" would be false.

### Failure modes the code keeps visible

- A negative review stays negative. Format recovery and runtime recovery do not turn it into a pass (`docs/runtime-recovery.md`).
- A failed independent unit blocks dependents. It does not authorize them.
- Stale, swapped, or altered evidence fails closed. Changed chapters cannot inherit old passes.
- The revision budget is bounded. Autonomous mode can continue inside that budget after `major_rewrite`; it does not silently reset attempts.
- An interrupt must stop the owned process tree before the lock is released. Unverified shutdown keeps the lock.
- An oversized undispatched request needs an explicit recovery. Resume alone does not swap in a newer engine.
- Literary gates can fail while Markdown and EPUB still export. That outcome is a disclosed failure, not a success.
