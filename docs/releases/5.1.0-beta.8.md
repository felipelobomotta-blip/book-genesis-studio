Your creativity. Your agent. Your book.

Book Genesis is an open-source writing workflow inside your existing agent. Describe an idea; the workflow plans, drafts chapters, runs simulated reader reviews and an editorial audit, performs bounded revisions, and exports Markdown and EPUB. Files and progress live in your book workspace. A finished file is not a promise of sales or of human literary approval.

## What changed since beta 7

- After a valid negative coordinated repair review, grouped creative repairs can accept viable units independently. Dependency hints come from conservative companion-proposal citations in that review, not from a shared issue ID. A failed unit blocks its dependents. Each accepted unit needs a fresh check of the current book and a fresh four-reader preference vote. New passes use schedule version 3. Passes already reserved keep their original schedule. Implementation: `skills/book-genesis/scripts/revision_partition.py`, `creative_groups.py`, `correction_groups.py`, `autonomous_book.py`.
- A stopped autonomous Codex book whose Phase 5 request was too large to dispatch can be archived and then given the current checkout by an explicit command. The command does not call a model, refund passes, or change the scoring policy. `launcher/recovery.py`, `skills/book-genesis/scripts/recovery_evidence.py`, `docs/runtime-recovery.md`.
- Recovery refuses a symlink or reparse point at the book or archive itself. Ancestor links, including the macOS `/var` alias and Windows 8.3 names, are resolved before the containment check.
- On Windows with Python 3.10, an interrupt during an attached native session reaches shutdown without waiting for the child to finish. `launcher/native.py` polls the process instead of using `Popen.wait(timeout=...)`, whose 3.10 grace period can become an unbounded Windows wait.
- Test-only CI fixture corrections from `475b71f30dfd1c838b1409e8780ab6d6c8067f21` are in this tree. They do not change the beta 7 production payload. Record: `docs/releases/beta7-ci-portability-20261026.md`.
- A synthetic comparison helper now binds its vote to the manifest for that call. That is a test fix. It does not change how a native reader votes.

## Evidence and limits

The beta 8 pilot imported existing Luna-high candidates for chapters 4, 8, 18 and 22 into an isolated evaluation copy. It accepted 4, 8 and 18 there and retained 22. The main manuscript hashes stayed unchanged. Both preference panels in that pilot were 3-to-1. The ledger's offline suite at that checkpoint was 694 tests. No whole-book score was recalculated. Source: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-independent-revisions-ledger.md` and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-independent-revisions.md`.

Historical beta 6 remains a revised and exported 24-chapter, 83,517-word draft with an automated literary score of 72.52/100 and unmet literary gates. Beta 7's native diagnostic preserved a blocking review. Neither result is a beta 8 book, and neither is a score above 90.

No complete beta 8 book above 90/100, no new human critical rating, and no sales result is claimed. Public directory approval is not established. Official partnership is not established. See the readiness assessment before any public wording is approved.
