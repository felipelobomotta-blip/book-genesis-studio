# Source-bound accusations in coordinated revision checks

Whole-book audits already separate observed events, derived conclusions and
uncertain causal routes. Coordinated creative-repair checks now carry the same
evidence discipline into their blocking findings. This extends the REA-inspired
source contract; it does not run REA against literature.

A blocking finding names an unresolved assigned ticket or an introduced
regression, the affected chapters, a short causal test and its limitations.
It must cite exact prose quotes from every affected chapter's original and
proposed versions. Supporting quotes can name the relevant companion or
contract source. Headings are not prose evidence. The controller verifies each
quote against its assigned passage and rejects swapped versions, invented
quotations and incomplete chapter coverage within the existing two format
attempts. It never fills in evidence for the reviewer.

An introduced-regression finding must quote at least one changed mechanism in
its affected chapters. An unchanged quoted mechanism can still support an
unresolved assigned ticket, but does not establish that the proposal introduced
it. Exact quotes do not prove semantic interpretation or that a changed sentence
caused a regression. These remain reviewer judgments. Instructions distinguish
unshown acquisition or custody from on-page impossibility, and distinguish a
new disclosure from a new event.

This implementation change does not claim that verdict was false, approve its

The existing group flow is unchanged: stage all dependent proposals, review
the complete assembled book, obtain four concealed preference reads only after
a passing repair check, and publish the authorized group atomically. A supported
negative judgment rejects the group. Malformed checks exhaust their original
bounded attempts and retain the accepted manuscript. Authentication failures,
source tampering and unverified calls continue to block delivery.

Tests cover accurate negative claims, invented quotes, version swaps, unchanged
mechanisms, affected-chapter coverage, headings and empty evidence. Existing
group tests exercise valid rejection, bounded retry, blind comparison, crash
recovery, complete export and completed resume. Native compatibility and full
book quality on this exact source remain outcomes to test.
