# Review the surviving creative proposals against the complete book

During native trial 42 on `93e5a6c`, 21 creative chapter tasks formed a single
coordinated group. Chapter 20's two proposals failed the existing word-count
validation. Twenty other candidates were valid outputs, but the controller
discarded all of them before correctness review or blind comparison. This
does not establish that those candidates were better: they were never assessed.

A partial creative group now keeps the failed or unchanged chapter's complete
original in place and assesses the composite book. The source-bound reviewer
still checks every proposed replacement's ticket resolution, causal dependencies,
preserved strengths and scope. If a proposed repair needs the missing companion
edit, it must be blocked. A retained member's assigned ticket remains unresolved;
its rejection, attempt evidence and original prose are preserved.

Passing that check still requires the existing four-reader, counterbalanced
comparison of both complete books and a decisive preference majority. Only the
changed, approved members are published and counted as revisions. All-invalid
groups keep every original without an additional review or preference poll.

Version 2 manifests bind retained outcomes and native evidence as well as actual
proposed replacements. Initial publication, interrupted recovery and receipt
resume before final outcomes verify
the complete reviewed canonical manuscript, including retained chapters. Version
1 packets and receipts remain verifiable without migration. No model, literary
threshold, calibration, attempt budget or revision-pass limit changes.

Large groups also receive bounded stable identifiers. The real 21-member group
would otherwise produce a 101-character adjacent-reader step, exceeding the
executor's unchanged 100-character limit. Short identifiers remain unchanged;
large identifiers include the chapter range and a digest of the full ordered
member list. The manifest still lists every assignment and source. This also
covers factual groups through the supported 100-chapter book limit.

Synthetic regression coverage includes one surviving candidate, a dependency
block, unchanged originals, all-invalid proposals, negative preference, tampered
failed attempts, stale retained canon, interrupted publication, completed export
and resume, and the existing version 1 full-group behavior. These tests validate
controller behavior, not literary quality. Real trial results stay in the external
QA ledger; no >90/100 or commercial claim follows from this patch.
