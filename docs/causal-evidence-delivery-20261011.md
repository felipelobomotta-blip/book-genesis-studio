## Literary application

The bounded exploratory pilot adapted REA's evidence discipline to two complete
public domain mysteries, two official modern excerpts and two generated
suspense drafts. It did not run REA as a literary tool or analyze all bestsellers.
Its source-bounded craft hypotheses are shipped in
`references/patterns/causal-evidence.md` inside the core skill.

Whole-manuscript audits now return causal traces for important knowledge and
the central promise. Each records its subject, recipient, story time,
acquisition/setup, later use/payoff, reader disclosure, observation/inference
basis, status and limitations. Only actual chapter prose IDs can establish
events. Planned canon and headings cannot stand in for on-page evidence.
Later reader disclosure does not imply later character acquisition. A plausible
unshown route remains unknown. A proved contradiction needs a negative audit
and a linked continuity/structural finding. Byte provenance and report shape
do not prove the critic's interpretation.

The writer gets optional causal craft knowledge without scores or repair
history. The editor gets the supported audit traces and existing exact tickets.
Repeated denial/disclosure exchanges are tested for a changed action and cost;
over-explanation is repaired at the redundant interpretation while preserving
the image, necessary facts and payoff chain. There is no new paragraph length,
dialogue percentage or stylistic quota. The generated auditor agent is rebuilt
from its source role.

Read-only source reviewers can use their existing second format attempt with
the exact host-contract errors. They receive no previous response, judgment or
scores. A valid negative result is final. Blind readers and anonymous comparison
voters continue to receive identical fresh packets without feedback on votes.
