## Corrections and the next test

The native-call guard now serializes existing CLI calls and exposes responses only after completion. Reader validation checks required structure and exact quotations. Export preflight checks the required phase evidence, chapter inventory and approved word range. A read-only progress helper now reconciles saved chapter numbers and deterministic prose counts before resuming and during complete export; pending or skipped earlier phases cannot qualify as complete. Technical proofreading belongs before final scoring.

The later instruction corrections require a brief-to-outline contract check before drafting, retain complete reader-role instructions on retries, and prohibit suppressing findings to obtain valid JSON. Reports obtained through such suppression are degraded even when their JSON validates. Architecture verification packets now explicitly exclude previous verdicts and repair history, and pending calls cannot be recorded as completed verification.

The quota-interrupted mixed-host attempt remains preserved. Its first Codex Luna High continuation proved isolated same-family critics but stopped after three partial architecture repairs, with zero chapters. That exposed repeated repair of individual rows, an event timestamp inferred from a chapter span, and an exact-anniversary assumption applied to an ordinary duration.

The updated workflow consolidates dependent plan repairs and states temporal precision explicitly. A further traced continuation obtained a fresh neutral architecture PASS, then reached five chapters and 14,096 deterministic prose words. It was stopped after 56 minutes when a fourth full repair was announced after three continuity rechecks. The model calls remained serialized with intact receipts and correct settings, but the book was not complete or exported. The supervisor also abbreviated reader-role packets and omitted one word while copying a writer response into a staged chapter; neither is relabeled as compliant preservation.

The next correction makes original prose transfer programmatic, with response, staged and canonical hashes. Phase 3 continuity checks have one cumulative budget of three editor dispatches per block, including retries; new findings or a resumed context cannot reset it. Narrow factual improvements require verified fixes, unchanged accepted/candidate/neighbor bindings, and no introduced contradiction. Preexisting or unrelated findings remain open warnings for the whole-book audit and revision, rather than repeatedly blocking all progress. Failed verification preserves the accepted text. Warning handoff never becomes a clean verdict or permission to weaken the author's brief. Phase 5 literary revisions keep the separate four-reader promotion requirement.

After these source changes, the portable checks and 135 tests passed again. A new empty-folder Luna High test is now running from the original single idea and declared novella contract. No chapters, reports, repair history or model responses from earlier attempts were copied into it. Its outcome is pending. The preceding continuations remain resumed runs with recorded version/host boundaries, not fresh uninterrupted acceptance successes.

Same-family isolated critics are reported at that independence level. A successful delivery would be evidence for the tested workflow and scope, not for every supported host or every book length. Simulated reader scores do not establish human readership, sales, bestseller quality or publication approval.
