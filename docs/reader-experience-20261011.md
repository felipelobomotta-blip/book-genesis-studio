# Earlier reader observations and original voice design

Trial 30's first chapter received four positive continuation votes but a failed
checkpoint: multiple readers stopped at a relationship explanation that repeated
what the preceding exchange had already shown. The same reports remembered the
wrong piano note, the hidden keys and wet footprints under a locked door. These
are simulated-reader opinions bound to the actual paragraphs, not human testing
or a claim about commercial success.

Inspection found two avoidable authoring constraints. Later draft writers read
all earlier prose but did not receive its completed reader observations. The
voice foundation also required one deliberately rough sentence per chapter,
hard paragraph limits and neutral narration when no comp passage was accessed.
The trial's generated voice artifact repeated those constraints despite declaring
its comparison evidence unavailable.

## Change

The controlled runner supplies a separate, optional earlier-reader view to later
draft writers. Each observation identifies its source chapter and passage and
retains the original report and catalog hashes. Before producing the view, the
controller verifies the complete assigned panel, completed original reader calls,
immutable evidence, exact raw responses and current source catalogs. A modified,
partial, unlogged or stale report blocks the handoff before a new writer call.

The view includes attention dips, confusion, memorable passages and strengths as
well as weaknesses. It excludes continuation votes, confidence, shared statistics,
grade commentary and acceptance outcomes. Observations are subjective reactions,
not new canon or instructions to resolve a mystery early. Raw reports remain
unchanged. The helper makes no model calls, revises no prose and spends or resets
no attempt budget. Current and future checkpoint reports are not draft history;
critics receive none of this view.

Foundation now demonstrates an original voice in rest, pressure and aftermath
benchmarks. External claims still require actual source passages; unavailable
comparison evidence remains explicit. Sample statistics are observations, not
universal writing limits. Narration can change cadence when a scene earns it.
Neither drafting nor revision is required to manufacture a clumsy sentence.

## Verification and limits

Seven negative and positive handoff tests pass, including altered aggregate and
raw reader responses, changed prose, partial panels and missing completed calls.
The two-chapter integration test verifies that later writers receive the view,
first writers and critics do not, and revision, export, completed resume and tamper
detection still work. These fixtures do not prove native literary quality.

A read-only extraction also validated 152 reactions from twelve original native
readers of trial 30's chapters 1, 3 and 6. It reused their original evidence and
left the running book untouched. This verifies compatibility with genuine saved
reader reports; it is not a new reading or a successful new book run.

The full repository suite passed all 479 tests in 479.868 seconds, and
`python runner/installer.py verify-suite` passed. Fresh native book acceptance
is still outstanding. The fixed grading, calibration, phase
order and bounded repair policies are unchanged. Trial 30 continues on its own
frozen source; these changes are prepared in an isolated checkout.
