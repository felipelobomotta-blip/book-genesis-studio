# Assumptions

- The book will be written in English (`en`).
- The genre is an adult supernatural medical thriller with elements of coastal gothic, mystery, and psychological suspense.
- The primary audience is adult readers who enjoy atmospheric horror-thrillers, haunted institutions, unreliable testimony, and emotionally grounded protagonists.
- The target length is approximately 85,000 words.
- The narrative will use close first person, likely in the present tense, following the nurse’s night shifts in an immediate, claustrophobic manner.
- The protagonist is an experienced night-shift nurse, competent under pressure but carrying unresolved guilt or grief connected to a past patient death.
- The hospital is located in a storm-prone coastal town where erosion, flooding, institutional neglect, and local secrecy shape the atmosphere.
- The demolished room was officially removed after a renovation or structural incident two years earlier.
- The pages are not merely a technical malfunction; they are the first visible sign of a concealed event involving the hospital, the former room, and one or more patients.
- The supernatural elements will remain ambiguous enough to sustain mystery while ultimately providing a satisfying explanation or emotional resolution.
- The protagonist will be drawn into the case because the pages contain information that could not plausibly be known by a random prankster or malfunctioning system.
- The story will balance procedural hospital detail with escalating supernatural dread.
- The title is not supplied; “Ward Zero” is proposed as a provisional working title.
- The commercial position is “high-concept supernatural thriller with a grounded frontline protagonist.”
- The book should feel atmospheric and distinctive without becoming so abstract that the central mystery, stakes, and reading experience are unclear.
