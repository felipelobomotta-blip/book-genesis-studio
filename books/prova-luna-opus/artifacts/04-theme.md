# Theme

## Central Theme

Institutions can survive mistakes by burying them, but people cannot heal until responsibility is made visible.

## Theme as a Question

What do we owe the dead and the living when telling the truth may endanger the institution that is supposed to protect them?

## Subthemes

- **Responsibility versus blame:** Individual workers can make mistakes, but systems determine which mistakes become inevitable and who is punished for them.
- **Care under pressure:** Genuine care requires attention, witness, and the courage to interrupt unsafe authority.
- **Erasure and memory:** A removed room, altered chart, or missing name cannot guarantee that suffering has disappeared.
- **Professional identity:** Competence is not obedience. Ethical practice sometimes means refusing an order.
- **Grief and repair:** Mourning becomes destructive when guilt is mistaken for accountability; repair begins when the full story is shared.
- **Institutional survival:** Keeping a hospital open matters, but preserving its reputation cannot matter more than the patients inside it.
- **The cost of silence:** Silence may offer temporary safety while transferring danger to the next shift, the next patient, and the next generation.

## Symbols

### Room 0

The demolished room represents whatever an institution removes from its official story. Its impossible pages make erased suffering active again.

### The Pager

The pager symbolizes duty, interruption, and the moral demand to respond. Its short messages also represent testimony reduced to fragments by bureaucracy.

### The Sea

The sea carries away physical evidence but returns what has been buried. It reflects grief, recurrence, and forces too large to control.

### The Flood Line

The mark left by storm water on hospital walls becomes a visible record of what the building has endured. It also measures how close the present is to repeating the past.

### Handover Notes

Handover language symbolizes collective memory. When altered, it turns care into concealment; when restored, it reconnects one shift to the next.

### The Demolition Dust

Dust from Room 0 appears in places where it should not be. It symbolizes the persistence of material and emotional residue after an official deletion.

### Light and Backup Power

The hospital’s failing lights distinguish visibility from safety. Backup power keeps machinery alive while revealing how little protection remains when the system is strained.
