# Emotional Curve

The emotional movement follows Mara from controlled competence, through destabilization and isolation, into moral clarity and qualified release.

## Block One: The First Page

**Chapters:** 1–4

**Start emotion:** Fatigued competence. Mara is tired but secure in routine, protocols, and the predictable demands of a night shift.

**End emotion:** Unease. A page from Room 0 contains an ordinary request from a room that no longer exists.

**Abandonment risk:** Mara fears that the hospital’s systems, colleagues, and eventually her own mind will leave her alone with an impossible responsibility.

**High-impact beats:**

- Mara triages a real emergency while receiving the first page.
- She confirms that Room 0 is absent from the current floor plan.
- A coworker jokes about legacy equipment, but Mara notices the page uses a patient-care phrase no outsider should know.
- She answers the page aloud, beginning the relationship with the presence.

## Block Two: The Missing Patient

**Chapters:** 5–8

**Start emotion:** Curiosity mixed with professional skepticism.

**End emotion:** Alarm. A second page names a patient missing from every accessible record and predicts a current patient’s deterioration.

**Abandonment risk:** Mara risks losing the trust of her team if she appears obsessive or unstable; Nia begins to fear that standing beside her will damage both their careers.

**High-impact beats:**

- Mara finds an archived floor plan showing Room 0.
- A patient’s symptoms match the warning in the page, establishing that the messages contain actionable knowledge.
- Ruth Calder refuses to discuss the room’s final night.
- Mara discovers that the demolished room was sealed after a storm evacuation, not simply removed during renovation.

## Block Three: The Hospital Pushes Back

**Chapters:** 9–13

**Start emotion:** Determination. Mara believes evidence will protect her if she gathers enough of it.

**End emotion:** Paranoia and isolation. Her records access is revoked, her charting is audited, and pages begin arriving on her personal device.

**Abandonment risk:** The institution threatens to abandon Mara professionally by questioning her fitness for duty; Nia may withdraw to protect her license.

**High-impact beats:**

- Elias frames his concern as support while warning Mara not to pursue Room 0.
- A page includes a private detail from Mara’s fatal shift two years earlier.
- Nia finds evidence that old handover notes were altered after the storm.
- Mara realizes someone living may be monitoring the same system as the presence.

## Block Four: The Past Repeats

**Chapters:** 14–19

**Start emotion:** Fear sharpened into vigilance.

**End emotion:** Betrayal. A current patient is placed in danger through a decision that repeats the original Room 0 failure.

**Abandonment risk:** Mara must choose whether to trust Nia and Jonah, knowing that involving them may expose them to retaliation or physical danger.

**High-impact beats:**

- A storm closes roads and compromises the hospital’s communications.
- A patient is moved away from life-saving equipment under an administrative order.
- Mara disobeys protocol narrowly enough to save the patient, but the decision puts her license at risk.
- Jonah identifies a surviving electrical route connecting the current paging network to the sealed section.
- The pages reveal that multiple patients were present in Room 0, not just the one Mara believed had died.

## Block Five: The Sealed Infrastructure

**Chapters:** 20–25

**Start emotion:** Betrayal and grief. Mara understands that her guilt was deliberately isolated from the larger failure.

**End emotion:** Terrifying clarity. She reaches the surviving section of Room 0 and finds the final evidence.

**Abandonment risk:** Mara may lose the last practical allies around her if she prioritizes proof over immediate rescue; the presence may also abandon her if she treats the dead as evidence instead of people.

**High-impact beats:**

- Mara, Nia, and Jonah enter the flooded service corridors during the storm.
- They find the original paper chart, an unfiled handover sheet, and equipment logs.
- The documents show that Elias and senior leadership concealed undocumented patients to avoid regulatory and financial consequences.
- Mara discovers that Ruth altered the report under coercion and that Mara’s own order was misrepresented.
- The presence pages a final emergency involving a living patient trapped in the compromised ward.

## Block Six: The Choice

**Chapters:** 26–30

**Start emotion:** Moral certainty under extreme pressure.

**End emotion:** Sacrificial resolve. Mara chooses the living patient while ensuring the evidence survives outside the hospital.

**Abandonment risk:** To act ethically, Mara must accept that the institution may abandon her, Elias may destroy her career, and the presence may not reward her with forgiveness.

**High-impact beats:**

- Elias attempts to seize the documents and directs blame toward Mara.
- Mara uses her clinical knowledge to expose the unsafe order in real time.
- Nia broadcasts the evidence and the emergency communications beyond Elias’s control.
- Jonah restores a temporary power route long enough to open the sealed ward.
- Mara saves the patient rather than pursuing the presence’s apparent final location.

## Block Seven: The Record

**Chapters:** 31–34

**Start emotion:** Exhaustion and uncertainty after the hospital crisis.

**End emotion:** Grief with release. The cover-up becomes public, Mara names her own actions accurately, and the pages stop.

**Abandonment risk:** After the immediate danger ends, Mara must live without the consuming purpose of the investigation and risk reconnecting with Jonah, Nia, and ordinary life.

**High-impact beats:**

- An external inquiry distinguishes Mara’s error from the administrators’ systemic concealment.
- Ruth gives her complete testimony.
- Mara and Jonah speak honestly about their mother and the night Mara was absent.
- Room 0 is officially acknowledged in the hospital record.
- The final page appears after the system is disconnected, containing only a patient’s name followed by: “RECEIVED.”

## Overall Emotional Shape

The curve rises from unease into alarm, falls into professional and personal isolation, climbs through betrayal and discovery, peaks at the rescue-and-exposure choice, and settles into grief transformed by witness. The supernatural mystery may retain one unresolved mechanism, but Mara’s emotional question receives a clear answer: she is responsible for what she chooses now, not guilty for every failure the institution placed on her.
