# Project Brief

## Original User Idea

A night-shift nurse in a coastal hospital starts receiving pages from a room that was demolished two years ago.

## Inferred Direction

An adult supernatural medical thriller set over a series of night shifts in an aging coastal hospital. The protagonist receives increasingly urgent pages from a demolished room, each one connected to a patient who officially never existed or whose death was misreported. Investigating the messages exposes a buried institutional crime while forcing the nurse to confront personal guilt about a fatal decision made during a previous shift.

The story should begin as a contained workplace mystery and gradually widen into coastal gothic horror. The hospital’s erased room becomes both a literal impossible location and a symbol of the institution’s ability to conceal suffering.

## Language

English (`en`).

## Genre

Adult supernatural medical thriller with coastal gothic and psychological suspense elements.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Adult readers of atmospheric horror and suspense.
- Readers who enjoy haunted hospitals, institutional secrets, investigative protagonists, and escalating supernatural mysteries.
- Fans of emotionally grounded thrillers in which professional competence is central to survival.

Secondary audience:

- Readers of contemporary gothic fiction.
- Readers drawn to stories about healthcare systems, moral injury, grief, and institutional cover-ups.

## Target Length

Approximately 85,000 words.

## Narrative Mode

Close first person, preferably present tense, from the night-shift nurse’s point of view.

The restricted viewpoint keeps the reader trapped inside the protagonist’s uncertainty. Clinical observations, pager messages, shift reports, and fragmented memories can provide controlled access to the mystery without weakening suspense.

## Provisional Title

**Ward Zero**

## Reader Promise

Every night, a nurse receives impossible pages from a room that no longer exists. To discover who is calling—and why—the nurse must navigate a haunted coastal hospital, uncover a patient the institution erased, and decide whether exposing the truth is worth risking the lives of the people still inside.

The reading experience promises:

- Immediate workplace authenticity.
- A high-concept supernatural hook.
- A steadily tightening mystery.
- A vividly threatening coastal setting.
- Emotional stakes rooted in grief, responsibility, and professional ethics.
- A resolved central mystery with room for unsettling ambiguity around the supernatural.

## Commercial Legibility

The premise is easy to communicate in one sentence: **a nurse receives pages from a demolished hospital room**. The hospital setting supplies a familiar, high-pressure environment, while the coastal location gives the book a distinctive visual and atmospheric identity.

The version should remain commercially legible through:

- A clear protagonist with a recognizable job and practical skills.
- A concrete mystery involving the demolished room.
- Escalating consequences across successive shifts.
- A human conspiracy or cover-up beneath the supernatural disturbance.
- A strong emotional question: what does the protagonist owe the dead, the living, and the truth?

The book should avoid generic haunted-house treatment by making nursing knowledge, shift culture, patient records, triage, and institutional power essential to the plot rather than decorative background.
