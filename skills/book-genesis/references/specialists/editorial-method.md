# Integrated editorial method

Original Book Genesis implementation informed by the competitive study dated 2026-10-25. These are craft methods, not evidence of sales, reader preference or guaranteed literary quality. Load only the section appropriate to the current role; blind readers receive none of this guide.

## Planning

Preserve the author's required events, excluded elements, audience, tone and reader promise. Separate explicit constraints from creative defaults. Express the concept as a premise, then expand its causal structure, character contradictions and scene work before committing to a long manuscript. For nonfiction, identify the reader's problem, prerequisites and concrete outcome rather than inventing a fictional plot.

In each chapter's **scene_contract**, name the focal person's goal, resistance, consequential choice, resulting change, cost and next consequence. Several scenes can share a chapter; record their linked actions compactly. Quiet reflection can change a decision or relationship without an external reversal. In **arc_change**, say what becomes possible, impossible or more costly and how this differs from the preceding chapter. Flag repeated confrontation/clue/deferral loops and information that never changes anyone's behavior. Do not impose a universal beat percentage or require every ending to be a cliffhanger.

In **knowledge_boundary**, distinguish what the viewpoint character knows before the scene, the source of a new acquisition, what other participants know, and what the reader learns here. Story time and disclosure time may differ. A later revelation can disclose an earlier event; planned or inferred knowledge is not demonstrated knowledge. Keep future outcomes out of early writer packets.

Before long drafting, put two original short voice samples in the existing voice artifact: one under pressure and one at rest. Show different habits of attention, syntax and dialogue for principal characters. If the author supplied their own prose, label the available corpus and its confidence; a small casual sample does not establish a book-length voice. Otherwise label the voice as an original design, not a measured imitation. Never claim to have analyzed unavailable books or author samples. The first chapter's reader checkpoint provides early feedback before later chapters are written.

For evidence-based nonfiction, establish a source register before drafting factual claims: actual accessed source, supported claim, locator and uncertainty. A plausible title or model memory is not a retrieved source. If source access is unavailable, label the claim unverified and preserve the gap; do not invent a citation. Memoir distinguishes the author's recollection, inference and independently sourced fact. Fictional world facts are not external-world evidence.

## Drafting

Write the scene's goal and conflict as lived action. Let choices incur costs and change what happens next. Preserve the author's concrete events rather than replacing them with commentary about the theme. Vary scene mechanisms and chapter endings according to the book; a quiet transition may be right. Avoid repeating the same confrontation to meet length.

The complete accepted chapters outrank provisional summaries. The source index and exact handoff locate that evidence; they do not certify every sentence as true. Keep separate: planned events; on-page observations; character beliefs; inference; reader disclosure; object custody. Current viewpoint knowledge must have a plausible acquisition route. Do not silently grant a character a secret just because the narrator or reader knows it. Unshown ordinary transfers remain uncertain unless the text excludes them.

Use the original voice samples and character cards as direction, not sentences to recycle. Characters should not all share the same verbal tic. End on the earned action, image or choice when it already conveys the meaning; an interpretive recap is optional and must add something. Do not enforce punctuation bans, mandatory rough sentences or universal paragraph ratios.

For nonfiction, write only the claims supported by supplied source material as sourced facts. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish examples from evidence. Never turn a missing source into an invented quotation, DOI, URL or bibliography entry. Do not claim live research from a tool-free packet.

## Revision

Diagnose before rewriting: source passage, specific problem, effect on the intended reader, and a concrete repair direction. Separate observation, inference and taste. A valid source ID establishes location; the cited action must still support the allegation. Preserve a negative finding when warranted, and preserve uncertainty when the source cannot decide it.

Prioritize causal/structural repair before polishing prose that may be replaced. For each repair identify setup, acquisition, use and payoff; follow the actual dependency across chapters. Group interdependent changes together. A general stylistic concern is not permission to attach every issue to every chapter. Scope each task to its evidenced occurrences and necessary consequences; never split a real dependency merely to pass a local check. Do not add chapters or change the author's contract silently.

For a prose/voice ticket, focus on a few supported recurring habits: shared character tics, interchangeable diction, repetitive image or scene mechanism, and explanatory endings after the action already communicates the point. Preserve intentional repetition, asymmetry and remembered strengths. Propose the smallest coherent scene/arc repair that fixes the defect. A correctness check and blinded original/candidate preference serve different purposes; one cannot substitute for the other.

Treat companion edits as proposals until the complete proposed book passes its required checks. A new proposal cannot prove old canon. Use current source fingerprints, retain previous attempts, and never reset the repair budget to get a favorable verdict. Export success and editorial acceptance remain separate.
