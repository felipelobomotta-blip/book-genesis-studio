# Causality and the middle of the book

Load for stories whose resolution depends on a mechanism, mystery, technology or supernatural rule: while seeding the continuity ledger in Phase 2, then during the full audit and targeted revision. Do not add a system encyclopedia to an ordinary story.

## Keep a small causal contract in the ledger

For each mechanism that changes the outcome, record:

| Rule | Trigger and prerequisites | Affected state | Cost or limit | Exception and setup | First demonstration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use the story's own terms. Distinguish two terms only when confusing them changes the plot: a name on a list may be different from a person arriving, for example. Anchor an event within its chapter's time span; do not treat the span's start as every event's timestamp. Mark planned facts as planned until prose demonstrates them, then quote the actual chapter and update dependencies together.

Track what each character learns and how before they act on it. A reader may not know the explanation yet; the underlying events and consequences still have to agree. Deliberate uncertainty about meaning is compatible with clear action and cost.

## Read the causal chain after drafting

The auditor reconstructs the decisive chain from the chapters, without supplying missing explanations from the ledger: trigger, choice, consequence, cost, final outcome. Any step supported only by the plan becomes a quoted setup or clarity ticket. Do not invent new rules in the final movement to repair a missing cause.

Test exceptions against earlier demonstrations. Keep a mystery when it is intentional; flag incompatible events, unexplained state changes or an ending that needs a new exception. Verify each quote before accepting the finding.

## Diagnose a stalled middle

Compare adjacent middle chapters by what changes: action, knowledge, relationship or available choice. Flag a repeated explanation or procedure only when it adds no meaningful change. Give the editor one or two supported blockers at a time and the passages readers remembered.

Prefer a consequential action, a failed attempt, a changed relationship, or a cut/merge to another paragraph explaining the rules. Preserve voice, ambiguity of meaning and quiet aftermath. A targeted repair must leave the causal chain clearer without flattening the book into an instruction manual.
