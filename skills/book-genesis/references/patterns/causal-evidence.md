# Causal evidence in narrative

Use this method to test a specific scene or repair. It is not a recipe for sales
and does not impose paragraph lengths, dialogue percentages or a universal beat
quota. The exploratory comparison that motivated it used two complete public
domain mysteries, two modern publisher excerpts and two generated suspense
drafts. Excerpts were not treated as complete books or representative samples.

## Acquisition, disclosure and use

Track a consequential fact as a chain: fact, recipient, acquisition event,
story time, later use and exact source passages. Keep disclosure to the reader
separate. A later chapter can reveal an earlier acquisition; chapter order alone
does not establish a contradiction. A character can infer a fact from shown
clues. A plausible unshown communication remains unknown rather than proof of
impossible knowledge. Explain what evidence would settle the uncertainty.

For a clue, record its first observable form, any reinterpretation and the
choice or consequence it enables. In Arthur Conan Doyle's *The Hound of the
Baskervilles*, the new boot in chapter 4, the old boot in chapter 5 and the
explanation in chapter 15 form a testable chain. In Agatha Christie's *The
Mysterious Affair at Styles*, the bolt evidence in chapter 4 is reinterpreted
through testimony and an experiment in chapter 12. These are selected examples
of craft, not evidence that the technique caused either book's success.

Sources: [The Hound of the Baskervilles](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2852),
[The Mysterious Affair at Styles](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/863).

## A repair must change the mechanism

When consecutive scenes repeat denial, questioning and disclosure, identify
what each scene changes. A repeated mechanism is a defect when it produces no
new choice, cost, obstacle or consequence. A recurring ritual can still earn
its place through a changed relationship. Keep that distinction in the ticket.

A structural ticket can replace the redundant exchange with an action whose
cost moves the story. Preserve the established clues, character knowledge,
calendar, object custody and payoff dependencies. Additional explanation cannot
substitute for a missing action. Stage every necessary setup and consequence
together, then let a fresh reviewer test the complete proposal before any
chapter is published.

For over-explanation, cite the image or action that already carries the meaning
and the sentence that restates it. Cut only the redundant interpretation. Keep
an explanation that supplies a new causal fact, character-specific mistake or
essential ambiguity. A concealed comparison with fresh readers tests whether
the revision preserves what made the scene memorable.

## Evidence discipline

The evidence discipline is adapted from [REA's evidence contracts](https://github.com/morluto/rea/blob/76e4153d346b14ef194222bbeef240cf8a16dc99/src/domain/evidence.ts):
separate observed source facts, derived conclusions and inferred hypotheses;
retain locations, hashes, limitations and unknowns. REA is a software reverse
engineering tool. This adaptation does not claim its CLI analyzes literature.
The host can verify passage identities and byte provenance; an independent
reader still has to assess the interpretation. Machine judgments do not prove
human satisfaction or bestseller potential.
