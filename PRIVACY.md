# Privacy

Effective 2026-10-26. Publisher: Felipe Lobo. Applies to the Book Genesis open-source plugin, not to the services of the agent, model provider, GitHub or a third-party distribution directory.

Book Genesis has no publisher-operated book backend, account database or analytics service. The plugin's Python helpers write manuscripts, plans, evaluations, prompts, model responses and execution receipts into the book workspace and its local execution folders. These records can include your idea, manuscript and other material supplied to the agent. Copies and frozen skill snapshots support continuation and verification. They remain until you remove them; uninstalling the plugin does not automatically erase book folders or host history.

To generate or review text, the host and configured model process the context provided to them. They may retain it according to their own policies and your account settings. Running locally does not imply offline inference or that manuscript text never reaches your selected provider. Third-party integrations you enable have their own data handling. Book Genesis does not ask users to paste account tokens into manuscripts.

You control which material and folders you give the host access to. Review its permissions, keep backups, and delete local book/run folders when no longer needed. The plugin cannot erase copies already held by your host, provider or backup service; use their controls separately.

If you open a public GitHub issue, the text and files you submit are public and handled by GitHub. Send only redacted examples. Support and privacy questions: https://github.com/felipelobomotta-blip/book-genesis-studio/issues. Do not include private material in a public issue.
