# Support

Book Genesis is a free, MIT-licensed beta maintained by Felipe Lobo.

For installation problems and reproducible bugs, open an issue at https://github.com/felipelobomotta-blip/book-genesis-studio/issues. Include the plugin version, operating system, agent/version, failed stage and a redacted error. Do not post tokens, complete private journals or unpublished manuscripts. There is no guaranteed response time or service-level agreement.

Start with [installation and troubleshooting](docs/plugins.md). For an interrupted book, keep its folder and ask your agent to continue from saved progress. Exported files and literary acceptance are separate outcomes. Host account, billing and quota problems belong to your chosen host's support service.
