# Book Genesis all-platform playbook

This is the operating plan for distributing Book Genesis across the major social, developer, and launch channels. It is a campaign system, not a promise that every platform will be used every day.

## One source, native adaptations

Create one evidence packet each week:

- one real workflow artifact;
- one short screen recording;
- one founder explanation;
- one limitation or failure and what changed;
- one invitation to try a first chapter.

Adapt that packet to each platform. Do not paste the same launch copy everywhere at once.

The recurring format is **one creative decision, one visible result, one lesson**.

Primary call to action:

> Try one chapter and tell me where the process breaks.

## Rules by channel

### X / Twitter

Lead with a concrete observation. Use a thread for the journey from idea to saved chapter. End with one link and one request. Avoid hashtag piles and repeated launch slogans.

### Threads

Use a warmer, slower version of the founder story. Ask a real question, such as: “What book idea have you kept postponing?” Reply manually to the first useful answers. Do not turn every reply into a pitch.

### LinkedIn

Show the engineering decision and its consequence. Good topics include the return to portable skills, the approval bug and its fix, a real resume test, and what a pilot user found confusing. Keep the beta status visible.

### Instagram

Use actual screenshots, recorded terminal frames, book covers, and short captions. A carousel should explain the workflow in six to eight slides. Reels must show a real action; do not manufacture an interface that the product does not contain.

### TikTok and Shorts

Start with the visual result within the first two seconds. Use captions. Keep one idea per clip: install, start a project, resume a project, or revise a weak paragraph. Label scripted sample data and synthetic narration when used.

### Reddit

Check the current community rules and recent moderator guidance before posting. Prefer answering a relevant question or using a community tool thread. If self-promotion is disallowed, do not disguise promotion as a neutral question.

### DEV and Hashnode

Write about the architecture: portable skills, saved state, host boundaries, editorial gates, and the difference between installer tests and literary acceptance. Include commands and a known limitation.

### Product Hunt and Hacker News

Use only after a real first-user journey is documented. Product Hunt needs a tested product page and real media. Hacker News requires an independently human-written submission and discussion; do not paste AI-generated comments.

## Weekly content loop

**Monday — evidence:** record or inspect one real workflow.

**Tuesday — explanation:** publish the founder or engineering lesson on LinkedIn and Threads.

**Wednesday — demonstration:** publish the native X thread and a short vertical cut.

**Thursday — conversation:** answer one relevant Reddit or developer-community question.

**Friday — visual recap:** publish an Instagram carousel and a YouTube Short.

**Weekend — measurement:** record visits, reported attempts, saved chapters, fresh-session resumes, and seven-day returns. Leave unknown values blank.

The global ceiling is two public placements per day and three substantive public replies per run. These are limits, not quotas.

## Approval and verification

The marketing agent drafts by default. Before an external post, show the complete copy, account, destination, link, asset, and disclosure. Felipe approves with:

```text
APPROVE: PLATFORM
```

After submission, verify the canonical public URL. A filled composer, a clicked button, or a success toast without a public page is not publication evidence.

## Minimum proof before a broad launch

- ten consenting users attempt the first-chapter journey;
- at least eight save a non-template chapter and resume in a fresh session;
- no unresolved data-loss or approval bypass remains;
- one complete short-book run is recorded with host, model, timing, chapter count, review, and revisions;
- two independent human readers review a permitted sample without seeing internal model scores.

Until those gates are met, the campaign should sell curiosity and invite testing, not claim bestseller production.
