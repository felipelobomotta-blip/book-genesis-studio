# The Archive Key

## Chapter 1

Friday, 8:00 a.m. Nora and Ivo stood inside the archive vault to inventory the last box. They had agreed not to open any sealed correspondence until both of them were present.

At 8:20, with Nora beside him, Ivo broke the seal on an envelope marked MAINTENANCE. He read the note aloud: "The spare key is sewn into the lining of the red coat in the upstairs cupboard."

Nora repeated the hiding place to make sure she had heard it correctly, then left for the pump house. Ivo folded the note back into the envelope and kept it in his pocket. Nora switched off her radio while working at the pump.

## Chapter 2

Friday, 8:40 a.m. Nora was at the pump house when Mae arrived with the tool bag. Water climbed the bottom step. Nora checked the pressure dial through her green safety goggles.

"Go upstairs in the archive," Nora told Mae. "There's a red coat in the cupboard. The spare key is sewn into its lining. Bring it here."

Mae put down the bag and ran toward the archive. Nora stayed with the pump, holding the switch in place with her thumb.

## Chapter 3

Friday, 9:00 a.m. Ivo reached the pump house and handed Nora the opened envelope. Mae had not yet returned.

Nora read the note herself, confirming the hiding place she had heard from Ivo in the vault forty minutes earlier.

"Stay here and hold this," she said. Ivo took the switch while she tried the pressure valve.
