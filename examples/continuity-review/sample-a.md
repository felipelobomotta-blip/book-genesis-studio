# The Archive Key

## Chapter 1

Friday, 8:00 a.m. Nora left the archive vault for the pump house. Ivo stayed behind to inventory the last box. They had agreed not to open any sealed correspondence until both of them were present.

At 8:20, alone in the vault, Ivo broke the seal on an envelope marked MAINTENANCE. Inside, a handwritten note read: "The spare key is sewn into the lining of the red coat in the upstairs cupboard."

Nora had never seen that note. Ivo had never seen it before either. Nora's radio was switched off, and no one carried a message between the vault and the pump house before 9:00. Ivo folded the note back into the envelope and kept it in his pocket.

## Chapter 2

Friday, 8:40 a.m. Nora was still at the pump house when Mae arrived with the tool bag. Water climbed the bottom step. Nora checked the pressure dial through her green safety goggles.

"Go upstairs in the archive," Nora told Mae. "There's a red coat in the cupboard. The spare key is sewn into its lining. Bring it here."

Mae put down the bag and ran toward the archive. Nora stayed with the pump, holding the switch in place with her thumb.

## Chapter 3

Friday, 9:00 a.m. Ivo reached the pump house and handed Nora the opened envelope. Mae had not yet returned.

Nora read the note. This was the first time anyone had told her where the spare key was hidden, and the first time she had seen those words.

"You opened it without me," she said. Ivo watched the rising water instead of answering.
