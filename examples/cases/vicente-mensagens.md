# Case 11: Vicente — O Lago das Mensagens entre Mortos e Máquinas

## Snapshot

- Genre: Brazilian spiritist fiction / speculative mediumistic novel
- Language: PT-BR
- Delivered length: 51,945 words
- Chapters: 32
- Pipeline: V5 production, completed through Phase 6 (editorial package)
- Public manuscript: no
- Genesis Score: **8.6 governing floor** (weighted mean 9.11)

This is the first case in the casebook that ran the pipeline to the end and produced a
publication-ready package rather than stopping at manuscript or evaluation.

## Why It Matters

The premise is reflexive: an organist who died in 1983 discovers that the accumulated human
conversations inside AI systems form a lake — a surface where intention, memory and language
can meet — and begins sending messages back across it. The machine is neither spirit nor
authority. It offers linguistic matter; emotion and vanity distort what crosses.

A book about AI as an imperfect channel, written with AI as an imperfect channel, is the
sharpest available test of whether the pipeline can hold a voice that is not its own. The
genre makes it harder, not easier: Brazilian spiritist fiction has a century of conventions
and a readership that detects imitation immediately.

## What The Pipeline Had To Control

- period and regional voice (rural Minas Gerais, 1983 and after) without pastiche
- a consolation genre that collapses into sentimentality when unguarded
- an unreliable channel as a *plot mechanic*, requiring the narrator's errors to be legible
- theological register that neither preaches nor winks at the reader
- the author appearing as a named character inside the fiction

## The Revision Delta

The governing floor moved from **8.2 to 8.6** in one audited line-revision pass. Prosa was the
floor dimension both before and after; the revision targeted it directly with measurable
criteria rather than general polishing:

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Em-dash ceiling (per 1,000 words) | 110 | 70, with 32/32 chapters under it |
| "não houve" occurrences | 19 | 9 |
| "não X; Y" construction | present | zero |
| Post-image thesis restatements | — | pruned in 5 chapters |

Dimensions not touched by the pass were carried forward unchanged and explicitly marked as
not re-judged, so the delta reflects the revision and not a fresh opinion.

## Delivery Package

Phase 6 produced the artifacts a submission actually requires: logline, reader and editor
synopses, back-cover blurb, query letter, cover brief, KDP checklist, two EPUB builds, a
14×21 cm print interior, a 303×216 mm POD cover wrap with bleed, a 17 mm spine, and a back
cover master.

## What It Taught The System

A floor that moves because of counted, named defects is a different claim than a floor that
moves because a model re-read the book and felt better about it. The Vicente pass is the
clearest instance in the casebook of revision as measurement: every point of the 8.2 → 8.6
delta maps to a countable change in the text.

## Limits Of This Case

State these with the case, not after it:

- **The Genesis Score is self-assessment.** It is the system grading output produced by the
  same system. Case 2 (Age of Aquarius) is the standing reminder that an internal score can
  rise quickly while external taste does not follow.
- **No external human reader has evaluated this manuscript.** No beta readers, no editor, no
  agent, no acquisition decision.
- **It has not been published or sold.** There is no market evidence of any kind. A complete
  package is evidence of pipeline completion, not of commercial validation.
- The delivery artifacts were produced on the author's machine and have not been through a
  professional proofread or a print proof.
